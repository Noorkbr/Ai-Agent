# Sample Knowledge Base — Demo Company: Nexus Support

## About Us
Nexus is a B2B SaaS platform that helps e-commerce brands automate their inventory management.
We serve over 500 businesses across North America and Europe.

## Pricing
- Starter Plan: $49/month — up to 500 products, 1 warehouse
- Growth Plan: $149/month — up to 5,000 products, 3 warehouses
- Enterprise Plan: Custom pricing — unlimited products, dedicated support

## Support Hours
Our human support team is available Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm EST.
Our AI agent is available 24/7 for instant answers.

## Refund Policy
We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on all plans.
To request a refund, email billing@nexusapp.com with your account email.

## Integrations
Nexus integrates with: Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon, eBay, QuickBooks, and Zapier.

## Getting Started
1. Sign up at nexusapp.com
2. Connect your e-commerce store via the Integrations tab
3. Import your product catalog (CSV or API sync)
4. Set reorder rules and alerts
5. You are live in under 30 minutes!

## Common FAQs

Q: Can I import products from a CSV file?
A: Yes! Go to Products then Import then Upload CSV. We support Shopify and WooCommerce export formats.

Q: Do you offer a free trial?
A: Yes, all plans come with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required.

Q: What happens if I exceed my product limit?
A: You will receive an email notification and can upgrade from Settings then Billing.
